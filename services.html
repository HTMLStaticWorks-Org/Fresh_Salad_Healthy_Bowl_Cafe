Weekly Delivery & Pickup Windows

Four convenient daily windows engineered around your workweek and training schedule.

Every bowl is assembled immediately prior to your selected window and packed in chilled, 100% plant-fiber compostable containers with dressings sealed on the side for maximum crunch.

  • Early Bird Doorstep (6:30 AM – 8:30 AM): Chilled insulated courier drop-off before you leave for work or morning workouts.
  • Midday Express Pickup (11:30 AM – 1:30 PM): Grab-and-go from our dedicated Subscriber Locker at the VERDURE cafe with zero wait.
  • Afternoon Desk Drop (1:30 PM – 4:00 PM): Direct office or studio courier delivery ideal for afternoon energy recharge.
  • Evening Dinner Drop (5:30 PM – 8:00 PM): Warm grain and roasted protein bowls delivered fresh for effortless weeknight dinners.
