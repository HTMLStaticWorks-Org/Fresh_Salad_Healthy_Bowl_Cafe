# VERDURE — Fresh Salad & Healthy Bowl Cafe

A fresh, light-first culinary website for **VERDURE — Fresh Salad & Healthy Bowl Cafe**, featuring interactive build-your-bowl customization, a menu organized by base and protein, flexible weekly meal plan subscriptions, and a comprehensive subscriber dashboard.

## Features
- **Home (`index.html`)**: Fresh, light botanical layout featuring build-your-bowl options, signature bowl previews, interactive 5-step Build-Your-Bowl nutrition customizer, subscriber reviews, and farm-to-bowl philosophy.
- **Home 2 (`home2.html`)**: Alternate culinary experience featuring 100% organic harvest pillars, interactive 5-step Farm-to-Bowl craft explorer, and culinary & dietitian team profiles.
- **Our Story (`about.html`)**: Culinary manifesto, zero-seed-oil cold-pressed dressing kitchen, gut-brain nutrition science, Regenerative Grower Fellowship, and the 1928 Botanical Conservatory flagship cafe.
- **Meal Plans (`services.html` & `tickets.html`)**: Weekly bowl subscription plans (3-Bowl Starter, 5-Bowl Workweek, 10-Bowl Performance), daily delivery & express locker pickup windows, Performance Plus upgrades, and dietary FAQs.
- **Menu by Base & Protein (`counselors.html` & `exhibits.html`)**: Signature bowls organized by organic bases (crisp leafy greens, warm sprouted grains, herb cauli-rice, 50/50 split) and clean proteins (herb chicken, wild king salmon, miso glazed tofu, grass-fed steak, crispy falafel, wild gulf shrimp) with complete macro badges.
- **Catering & Corporate Plans (`contact.html` & `groups.html`)**: Office Build-Your-Own Bowl Bars, labeled group macro boxes, corporate meal plan subsidies, flagship cafe map, and catering FAQs.
- **Nutrition Journal (`blog.html`)**: Dietitian macro coaching articles, gut microbiome guides, and downloadable allergen & dressing recipe books.
- **Subscriber Dashboard (`dashboard.html`)**: Subscriber portal for choosing weekly bowl plans, pausing or skipping deliveries, viewing complete nutrition & macro labels, tracking subscriber perks, managing payments, and updating dietary/allergen preferences.
- **Subscriber Login Portal (`login.html` & `register.html`)**: Dedicated authentication portal for meal plan subscribers.
- **Light & Dark Theme + RTL Support**: Fresh, light botanical theme by default (`data-theme="light"`) with dark forest mode toggle and full RTL layout support.
