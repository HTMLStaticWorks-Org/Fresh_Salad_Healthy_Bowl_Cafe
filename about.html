About VERDURE

Culinary Manifesto

The Genesis: Beyond Fast-Casual Compromise

Fast, nourishing food elevated from wilted convenience into a living celebration of peak-season harvests.

Founded in 2021 by a cross-disciplinary team of fine-dining plant-forward chefs, regenerative agronomists, and registered performance dietitians, VERDURE was conceived as a direct antidote to ultra-processed weekday lunches. We asked a fundamental question: What if fast-casual bowls were held to Michelin-level ingredient standards?

Sustainable Kitchen

Zero Seed Oils & Regeneratively Grown

Uncompromising ingredient purity paired with closed-loop environmental stewardship.

Behind VERDURE's vibrant flavors is a strict culinary charter developed alongside local organic farms and soil scientists. We banished refined canola, soybean, and corn oils—as well as artificial gums and refined sugars—from every dressing, marinade, and roasting pan.

  • 100% Extra-Virgin & Avocado Oils: Every house vinaigrette and roasting glaze is crafted exclusively with cold-pressed California olive oil, avocado oil, and raw stone-ground tahini.
  • Closed-Loop Composting & Glass Swap: All kitchen trimmings return to our partner farms as organic compost, while subscriber glass bowls eliminate single-use waste.

Nutritional Science

Gut-Brain Nutrition & Sustained Energy

Balancing prebiotic plant fibers, clean amino acids, and slow-release carbohydrates to eliminate the afternoon crash.

VERDURE works alongside clinical nutritionists and metabolic researchers to study how meal composition impacts cognitive focus and glycemic stability. Rather than spiking blood sugar with heavy refined starches, our bowls are calibrated for steady, all-day mental clarity.

  • 30+ Weekly Plant Varieties: Diverse polyphenols, fermented veggies, and sprouted seeds clinically shown to nourish the gut microbiome and support immune resilience.

Cafe Design & Space

The Sunlit Botanical Conservatory

From a historic 1928 brick conservatory into a bright, plant-filled culinary sanctuary.

Our flagship VERDURE cafe is housed inside a revitalized 1928 glass-and-timber botanical conservatory. Bathed in natural daylight and surrounded by living herb walls, warm white oak communal tables, and an open cold-bar kitchen, the space invites you to slow down and savor every bite.

  • 360-Degree Open Harvest Line: Watch our chefs char proteins to order, toss organic greens, and whisk fresh dressings in full view.
  • Express Subscriber Pickup Lockers: Temperature-controlled smart cubbies allow Meal Plan subscribers to scan their phone and grab their custom bowl in under 10 seconds.
  • Living Culinary Herb Walls: Vertical hydroponic towers inside the dining room grow the fresh basil, mint, cilantro, and micro-arugula snipped directly onto your bowl.
