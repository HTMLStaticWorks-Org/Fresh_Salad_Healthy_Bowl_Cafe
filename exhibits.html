How to Customize Any Bowl by Base & Protein

Follow three effortless steps to tailor any signature bowl to your dietary and macro goals.

01

Choose your base split

Select 100% crisp leafy greens, 100% warm sprouted ancient grains, low-carb turmeric cauliflower rice, or a half-and-half split.

02

Pair your clean protein

Pick herb-roasted chicken, wild salmon, grass-fed steak, wild shrimp, maple-tamari tofu, or baked herb falafel — or double up for recovery days.

03

Select dressing & crunch

Finish with one of our seed-oil-free cold-pressed dressings tossed light, medium, or packed on the side for your weekly meal plan box.
